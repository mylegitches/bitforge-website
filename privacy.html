Privacy Policy

Effective date: March 2026

BitForge AGI ("Bitforge," "we," "us," or "our") operates the website bitforgeagi.com. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you visit our site, how we use it, and what rights you have regarding your information.

If you have questions about this policy, contact us at adam@bitforgeagi.com.

1. Information We Collect

We collect two categories of information:

Automatically collected data. When you visit bitforgeagi.com, our analytics service automatically collects technical information about your visit, including:

  • Pages viewed and time spent on each page
  • Browser type and version
  • Operating system
  • Approximate geographic location (country/region, derived from IP address)
  • Referring URLs (which site or link brought you here)
  • JavaScript errors and performance metrics

This data is collected through Microsoft Azure Application Insights (see Section 4). We do not collect your IP address directly; it is used only by Application Insights to derive approximate location and is not stored by us.

Information you provide. If you submit our contact form, we receive the information you enter (such as your name, email address, and message). This information is used solely to respond to your inquiry.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Understand how visitors use our website so we can improve it
  • Monitor for errors and technical issues
  • Measure the performance and reach of our pages
  • Respond to contact form submissions

We do not use your information for advertising, automated decision-making, or profiling.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data to third parties. We do not share your data with any third party except as described in Section 4 (our analytics provider) or as required by law.

4. Third-Party Services: Microsoft Azure Application Insights

We use Microsoft Azure Application Insights to collect site analytics and monitor performance. Application Insights is a service provided by Microsoft Corporation. Data collected through Application Insights is transmitted to and stored on Microsoft's servers in accordance with Microsoft's Privacy Statement.

Application Insights may set cookies in your browser to track sessions and distinguish between returning and new visitors. These are functional analytics cookies; they are not used for advertising purposes.

We do not control Microsoft's data handling practices. By using this site, you acknowledge that analytics data may be processed by Microsoft.

5. Cookies

Our site uses cookies set by Microsoft Azure Application Insights for analytics purposes. These cookies help us understand usage patterns and improve the site. They do not track you across other websites or serve advertisements.

You can disable cookies in your browser settings. Doing so may affect site functionality or our ability to understand aggregate usage patterns, but will not prevent you from accessing any content on this site.

6. Data Retention

Analytics data collected through Application Insights is retained in accordance with Microsoft's default retention settings (90 days by default). Contact form submissions are retained only as long as necessary to respond to your inquiry.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights regarding your personal data, including the right to access, correct, or request deletion of information we hold about you. Because most data we collect is anonymized analytics, we may have limited ability to identify or retrieve data tied to a specific individual.

To exercise your rights or make a data-related request, contact us at adam@bitforgeagi.com. We will respond within a reasonable timeframe.

8. Children's Privacy

This website is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. External Links

Our site may contain links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites and encourage you to review their privacy policies before providing any personal information.

10. Security

We take reasonable technical measures to protect information collected through this site. However, no transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee the absolute security of your data.
