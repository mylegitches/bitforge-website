What to include in your message

To help us resolve your request as quickly as possible, please include:

  • Your company name and the name of your primary Bitforge contact, if known
  • A clear description of the issue or question
  • Any relevant deliverable name, system, or engagement reference
  • Screenshots or error messages, if applicable

Response times

Request type Typical response
General inquiries Within 1 business day
Active engagement support Within 4 business hours
Urgent / production-impacting As soon as possible — call directly for fastest response

For active engagements

If you are a current client with an active Statement of Work, your assigned Bitforge Delivery Lead remains your primary point of contact for day-to-day questions. The contact information above is available as an additional channel for general support, billing questions, or if you are unable to reach your assigned contact.

Security concerns

If you believe you have discovered a security vulnerability or data issue related to Bitforge's work, please contact us immediately using the information above and mark your message as urgent. We take all security reports seriously and will respond on an expedited basis.

To report an ethics, compliance, harassment, or other concern anonymously, use our Confidential Reporting Form. This form does not collect your name or email address.
